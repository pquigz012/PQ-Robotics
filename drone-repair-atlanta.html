What we fix

Most of what lands on the bench is crash damage, and most of it is repairable. If your drone hit something, got wet, or just stopped behaving, it's worth a look before you replace it. A repair is almost always a fraction of a new airframe.

  • Crash damageCracked arms and shells, bent or snapped booms, sheared gimbal mounts, damaged landing gear.
  • Gimbal & camera faultsDrifting or stuck axes, gimbal motor overload errors, torn ribbon cables, blurred or cracked lenses, failed calibration.
  • Water damagePond, pool and rain recoveries. Bring it in without powering it on — that's the difference between a repair and a write-off.
  • Motors & ESCsGrinding or seized motors, burnt ESCs, uneven thrust, drones that drift or flip on takeoff.
  • Flight controllersBoard-level diagnosis and repair, IMU and compass faults, sensor calibration failures, boot loops.
  • Firmware & tuningFailed updates, bricked recovery, Betaflight flashing, PID tuning, Remote ID compliance.
  • Batteries & powerSwollen or unbalanced packs, cells that won't charge, charging port and power distribution board repair.
  • Won't take off or connectNo-fly errors, transmitter binding problems, GPS lock failures, aircraft that arm but won't lift.
  • Custom builds & upgradesBespoke airframes built to payload, range or endurance spec, plus performance upgrades to what you already fly.

Work order

How a repair runs

  1. Drop off or ship it

    Walk into the Sandy Springs shop during business hours, or mail your aircraft in from anywhere in Georgia. Tell us what happened — even "I don't know, it just fell" is a useful starting point.

  2. Free diagnosis

    Full systems check: motor test, ESC calibration, sensor and firmware review, and a physical damage assessment. You get a written quote and a plain-English explanation of what failed and why.

  3. You decide

    No work starts without your approval, and there's no charge if you walk away. If the repair costs more than the drone is worth, we'll say so instead of quoting it.

  4. Repair, calibrate, test

    We fix it, recalibrate the sensors, update firmware where appropriate, and test fly before it goes back to you. It leaves ready to fly, not ready to troubleshoot.

Common questions

Before you bring it in

How much does drone repair cost in Atlanta?

It depends entirely on what broke. A prop and arm replacement is a small job; a gimbal or flight controller failure is a bigger one. Diagnosis is free and the quote is written, so you'll know the number before committing to anything.

Can a crashed drone actually be repaired?

Usually, yes. Crash damage tends to concentrate in the arms, gimbal mount and landing gear, all of which are replaceable. Even aircraft that look badly broken are often economical to fix. The main exception is severe water damage that's already been powered on.

My drone went in the water. What should I do right now?

Do not power it on and do not try to charge it. Remove the battery if you can do it safely, and bring the aircraft in as soon as possible. Corrosion is what kills waterlogged drones, and it starts immediately — the sooner it reaches the bench, the better the odds.

Do you repair DJI drones?

Yes — Mavic, Mini, Air, Phantom, Inspire and Avata platforms, including gimbal, camera, motor, ESC, battery and firmware work. We are an independent shop, not a DJI warranty center, so if your aircraft is still under warranty it's worth checking that route first.

Do you work on FPV and custom builds?

That's a large share of what we do. Betaflight flashing and PID tuning, ESC and stack repair, soldering and rewiring, plus builds specified from scratch around a payload, range or endurance requirement.

How long does a repair take?

Simple repairs often turn around in a few days. Anything waiting on a specific part depends on availability, and we'll give you a timeline with the quote rather than after.

Can I mail my drone in?

Yes. We accept mail-in repairs from across Georgia and the surrounding states. Call first so we can walk you through packing it — how a drone is boxed makes a real difference to whether it arrives in the same condition it left.
