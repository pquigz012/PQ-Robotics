Questions

Where can I get DJI Mini parts in Atlanta today?

At PQ Robotics, 6059 Boylston Dr NE, Atlanta, GA 30328. We keep DJI Mini 4 Pro, Mini 5 Pro and Mini 3 Pro arms, RC 2 and RC-N controllers, Mini 4 Pro batteries and charging hub, front arm axis springs and gimbal rubber dampers on the shelf. Reserve online or call (404) 408-9063 and pick up at the shop.

Do you have DJI Mini 4 Pro replacement arms in stock?

Yes. Mini 4 Pro arms with motors are $59 per arm, any position (front left, front right, rear left, rear right). We also stock Mini 5 Pro arms ($110 front, $95 rear) and Mini 3 Pro arms ($69 front, $49 rear).

Can you install the part for me?

Yes. The Drone Hospital is in the same shop. Add a note to your reservation or call (404) 408-9063 and we will quote the repair.

Are the parts new?

The Antigravity A1 and the gimbal rubber dampers are new. The other parts are used and working. Each card is marked Used or New.

Do I need a license to fly the A1?

Flying for fun, you need the free FAA TRUST certificate. Because the A1 weighs under 250 grams, recreational pilots do not have to register it. Flying in the goggles means you can't see the drone yourself, so bring a friend standing next to you as a visual observer. If you'll be paid for photos or video, you need a Part 107 license, and we teach that too.

Can you show me how to fly it?

Yes. Book a First-Flight lesson and bring your A1. We set it up with you and teach on your own drone.

What if I crash it?

Bring it to the Drone Hospital, our repair shop at the same address. Call (404) 408-9063 and tell us what happened.

I'm buying it as a gift. Anything else I should add?

A First-Flight lesson gift certificate pairs well with it, so they learn to fly on day one. Mention the gift in the order notes.

Can I return it?

Yes, unopened and complete within 14 days of pickup or delivery, for a full refund of the purchase price. Opened units are covered by Antigravity's warranty for defects, and we help you with the claim.

Can I pay with a credit card?

Not online yet. Right now we take Zelle, Venmo and checks. Call (404) 408-9063 if you need another option.
