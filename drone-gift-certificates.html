Common questions

About gift certificates

How does the person I'm gifting book their lesson?

They call or text (404) 408-9063 with the code on their certificate, and we set a time that works. Lessons start at our Sandy Springs shop or on-site at their location.

How long is a gift certificate good for?

Five years from the date it is issued. The price shown on the certificate is locked in, even if our prices change.

Do they need their own drone?

No. Students can fly our training drones. If they got a drone as a gift, bring it along and we will set it up and teach on it.

Is there an age limit?

Tell us the student's age when you order and we will suggest the right gift.

Can they switch to a different lesson or course?

Yes. A certificate can be used toward any PQ Robotics Education service or Drone Hospital repair of equal value. If they choose something that costs more, they pay the difference when they book.

When will I get the certificate?

Within 24 hours of receiving payment, by email, as a printable PDF. If you picked a send date, we hold it until then. Mailed checks are processed when they arrive.

Does the Part 107 gift include the FAA exam?

No. The FAA knowledge exam is paid to the testing center when the student schedules it. Our prep covers everything on the test, and in-person students can sit in on another class free if they don't pass.

Can I pay with a credit card?

Not online yet. Right now we take Zelle, Venmo and checks. Call (404) 408-9063 if you need another option.
