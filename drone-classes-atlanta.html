Common questions

Before your first lesson

Do you offer drone lessons in Atlanta?

Yes. PQ Robotics Education teaches private and small-group drone flying lessons, a week-long beginner course, guided custom builds, and drone classes for schools and organizations across metro Atlanta. Lessons start at our Sandy Springs shop or on-site at your location.

I just bought my first drone. Can you teach me to fly it?

Yes, and it is the best time to start. A lesson before your first real flight covers setup, safe takeoffs and landings, and what to do when something goes wrong, which is how most new drones get damaged.

What is in the week-long drone course?

Flight Week covers drone basics in five days, with a short ground lesson each day and most of the time spent flying. It starts at first hover and ends with a final check ride. The full syllabus is shared with enrolled students.

Can you help me build my own drone?

Yes. In a guided build you choose the type of drone, such as FPV freestyle, cinewhoop, long range, camera or autonomous, and we walk you through parts selection, soldering, assembly, firmware setup and the first flight. You keep the drone.

Can you teach a drone class at our school or Scout troop?

Yes. We run drone workshops, multi-week courses and build programs for schools, STEM clubs, Scout troops, camps and other organizations, on-site or at our shop. Programs are built around your group's age, size and schedule.

Do you sell DIY drone kits for classrooms?

The PQ DIY Drone Kit is coming soon. It includes the parts to build a working drone and a step-by-step build guide. Schools and organizations can join the waitlist through the form on this page.

Do I need an FAA license to fly a drone?

Recreational pilots need to pass the free FAA TRUST test and register any drone over 250 grams. Flying for any business purpose requires an FAA Part 107 remote pilot certificate. We cover which rules apply to you during your lesson. We also teach Part 107 prep classes in person in Sandy Springs and live online for all of Georgia.

How much do drone lessons cost?

A 90-minute First-Flight Lesson is $175, private coaching is $110 an hour, Flight Week is $595 per person, guided builds start at $349 plus parts, and school workshops start at $550.
