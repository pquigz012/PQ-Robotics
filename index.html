About Us

Experience and capabilities

Our development work draws on a B.S. in Intelligent Systems Engineering from Indiana University, experience building custom drone platforms for military applications, and PhD-level engineering knowledge across hardware, autonomy and software integration.

Our team covers the full engineering stack: airframe design, flight controller configuration, sensor integration, firmware and cross-system compatibility. We have built autonomous and manually piloted aircraft from the ground up, from FPV platforms to multi-rotor hexacopters.

The same expertise supports our drone repair and education services. Our qualified DJI technicians have diagnosed and repaired hundreds of DJI aircraft, gimbals and accessories, and we provide training and lessons, custom builds and aerial cinematography.
