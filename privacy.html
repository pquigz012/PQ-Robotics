Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information PQ Technologies LLC, doing business as PQ Robotics and Drone Hospital ("we", "us"), collects through pq-robotics.com and how we use it. We keep it simple: we collect only what we need to answer you and do the work, and we don't sell your information.

Information you give us

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, the service you're interested in, and your message.
  • Calls and texts. Your phone number and anything you tell us, so we can respond.
  • Repairs and lessons. Details you provide about you and your aircraft or training needs, so we can quote, perform and follow up on the work.

Information collected automatically

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Our website host, GitHub Pages, may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

Some pages load content from other services, which receive basic technical information such as your IP address when they do:

  • Google Fonts for the site's typefaces.
  • YouTube for videos on the footage page. YouTube may set its own cookies when a video loads or plays.
  • National Weather Service for the flight conditions on Drone Hospital Daily.
  • Formspree, which delivers contact form messages to us.

Links to Instagram, Google Maps and news publishers take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Saved in your browser

If you choose topics to follow on Drone Hospital Daily, that choice is saved in your own browser's storage. It isn't sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes it.

How we use information

  • To reply to you, provide quotes, and schedule and perform repairs, training and other services.
  • To keep records of work performed and to meet legal or tax obligations.
  • To keep the website secure and working.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our form, phone and hosting providers, or when required by law. We do not sell or rent personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service and maintain business records, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Call or text us using the details below.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. Parents and guardians handle arrangements for any lessons involving minors.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
